3. Cookies

The service itself uses only cookies that are strictly necessary (keeping you signed in). Our website may use analytics and advertising cookies only after you accept them in the cookie banner; you can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings". Details are in the Cookie Policy.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and restaurant data: for as long as the account exists. It is deleted right away if you delete your account.
  • Billing records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Logs: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted automatically within a few days.

6. Guests of restaurants

When someone scans a restaurant's QR code, we don't ask for their name, phone number or an account. We store the dishes, the table, the time, the language and optional notes. This data belongs to the restaurant, which decides how it is used. For requests about it, contact the restaurant; we help it respond.

7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored with scrypt, data kept separate per restaurant, each restaurant on its own address, limits on sign-in attempts, hosting in the EU and regular backups.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, download and delete your data from Admin → Subscription, or by writing to us at . Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or New York), you may have additional rights, such as knowing what we collect and opting out of targeted advertising. We answer within 30 days and we never treat you differently for using your rights.

9. International transfers

The service is run from the European Union, and data is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Transfers to providers in the United States follow the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will tell you by e-mail before the change applies.
