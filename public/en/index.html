How it works

From sign-up to your first order in one afternoon

Everything happens online, from your computer or phone. No installation, no app for your guests and no new hardware.

Create your account

With your e-mail, in a minute. You start with a sample menu in 8 languages and change dishes and prices.

Print your QR codes

One for every table, room or lounge chair: stickers, table tents or cards, with your logo.

Take orders

Servers approve them on their phone and the kitchen sees them on a screen or prints them.

For your guests

Order without waiting for the server

Guests see when their food will be ready, call the server with one tap and ask for the check from the table.

  • Dish notes and choices like "medium rare" or "extra feta +$3"
  • Allergens and dietary tags on every dish
  • Pay at the table from the phone: coming soon in the US
For the kitchen and the bar

Every station sees only its own tickets

Drinks go to the bar, food to the kitchen. A timer on every ticket, printing on a thermal printer and an alert when it is ready.

  • Runs on any tablet or computer
  • Guests see the estimated time
  • Pick-up orders with a code
Your brand

A menu that looks like your place

Design the whole experience yourself, no designer needed. Every change shows up right away in the preview and on your guests' phones.

  • Logo and cover photo on the menu, the QR cards and the browser tab
  • Colors for background, text, categories and buttons, always readable
  • Five typefaces and ready-made themes, from a classic bistro to a dark lounge
For your team

Servers see everything on their phone

Calls, new orders and checks per table. Each server signs in with their own PIN and sees their section first.

  • Approve orders before they reach the kitchen, or send them straight in
  • Take an order at the table, fix a mistake, move a party to another table
  • Works next to your POS: ring up the check as you do today

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer? Write to us and we'll get back to you the same day.

Do I need to install anything, or have someone come over?

No. You sign up online, set up your menu in the browser and print the QR codes on your own printer or at a print shop. Servers sign in from their phone with a link and a PIN.

How does the free trial work?

For 14 days you get every feature, without a credit card. If you don't continue, the menu stops showing to guests, but your menu, QR codes and history stay saved and work again as soon as you subscribe.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code with the camera and the menu opens in the browser.

Can guests pay from their phone?

Not yet in the United States: it is coming soon, with card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Today guests order and ask for the check from the phone, and you close the check on your POS as you do now.

Does it replace my POS?

No, it works next to it. Orders can go through the server, who rings them up on your POS as today. The check copy from Kalimenu is not a sales receipt; you can note your POS receipt number on it.

We are seasonal. Do we pay all year?

No. Pause your subscription in the months you are closed, free of charge. Your menu, QR codes and history stay saved and you continue with one click.

Do I need a tablet or a printer?

Not necessarily. Servers work from their own phone. For the kitchen any tablet or computer is enough, and if you have an 80 mm thermal printer it prints tickets too.

Can I change dishes and prices myself?

Yes, from the admin, whenever you like. Changes show up right away, without reprinting the QR codes.

Which languages does the menu speak?

English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Greek, Dutch and Polish. The menu opens in the language of the guest's phone, and you can hide the languages you don't need.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay monthly and stop whenever you like. Pay yearly and you get 2 months free.
