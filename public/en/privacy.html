5. How long we keep it

  • Account and restaurant data: for as long as the account exists. It is deleted right away if you delete your account.
  • Billing records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Logs: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted automatically within a few days.

6. Guests of restaurants

Guests who scan a restaurant's QR code don't need an account. For the restaurant we store:

  • Orders from the table: the dishes, the table, the time, the language and optional notes. No name or phone number.
  • Pick-up orders: also a name, a phone number and the pick-up time, so the restaurant can prepare and hand over the order.
  • Feedback and loyalty card: the rating and optional comment, and a random code of the device so visits can be counted, with nothing that identifies the guest.

On the guest's device we keep only the language, the cart and that random code (browser storage, no tracking cookies). This data belongs to the restaurant, which decides how it is used; we process it on the restaurant's behalf (data processing terms) and keep it as long as the restaurant keeps its order history or its account. For requests about it, contact the restaurant; we help it respond. We do not sell guests' data or use it for advertising.

7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored with scrypt, data kept separate per restaurant, each restaurant on its own address, limits on sign-in attempts, hosting in the EU and regular backups.
