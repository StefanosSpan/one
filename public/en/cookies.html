Cookie Policy

Version of September 29, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

1. What cookies are

Small files your browser stores when you visit a website. Some are necessary for it to work (for example to keep you signed in); others are used for analytics or advertising, only if you agree.

2. Necessary (always on)

NamePurposeDuration
staffKeeps the owner or staff signed in on the restaurant's addressUp to 30 days
superKeeps the service administrator signed in12 hours
kalimenu_consentRemembers your cookie choice6 months

The browser also keeps some preferences locally (local storage), such as the menu language, the cart, the restaurant code and a random number for the loyalty card. They are not sent to third parties.

3. Analytics (only with your consent)

When enabled, Google Analytics measures anonymously which pages of the website are visited (cookies _ga, _ga_*, up to 2 years). Provider: Google LLC.

4. Advertising (only with your consent)

When enabled, the Meta Pixel measures how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform (cookie _fbp, 3 months). Provider: Meta Platforms, Inc.

5. Where there are no analytics or advertising cookies

The menu that restaurant guests open from the QR code and the staff screens use necessary cookies only.

6. How to change your choice

Use Cookie settings, at the bottom of every page of the website, or your browser settings, where you can also delete cookies. Withdrawing consent applies from the next page you open. You can also use the Global Privacy Control signal of your browser: we treat it as a refusal of advertising cookies.
